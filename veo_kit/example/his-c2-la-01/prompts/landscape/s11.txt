The man from the reference images, in his plain black short-sleeve polo shirt with a cream cotton cloth over his left shoulder, stands on the stone steps of a busy river landing where cargo boats are tied up. He takes a heavy clay jar passed up from the boat, hands it on to the next man, then turns to the camera. He says in Hindi, in a warm, clear, unhurried teacher's voice: "आज सड़कें हैं, पर उस जमाने में नदियाँ ही रास्ता थीं। नदी से सस्ता और तेज़ कुछ नहीं।" He finishes, closes his mouth and gestures along the line of boats. A chain of porters passes jars up the steps around him. His is the only voice. Voices at a distance, water and footsteps. Natural, true-to-life colour; a clean, uncluttered documentary frame.
