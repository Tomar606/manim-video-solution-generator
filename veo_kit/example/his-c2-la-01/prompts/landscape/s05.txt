Medium shot, the camera holding still. The man from the reference images, in his plain black short-sleeve polo shirt with a cream cotton cloth over his left shoulder, stands at the edge of the rice field beside a loaded bullock cart, grain sacks stacked on it. He heaves one more sack up onto the cart with both hands, dusts off his palms and turns to the camera. He says in Hindi, in a warm, clear, unhurried teacher's voice: "सोचो, ज्यादा अनाज मतलब ज्यादा लोगों का पेट भरना। और ज्यादा लोग मतलब बड़ी सेना।" He finishes, closes his mouth and steps aside as the cart begins to roll away towards distant tents. A farmer holds the bullocks' rope. His is the only voice. The creak of the cart and the low of the bullocks. Natural, true-to-life colour; a clean, uncluttered documentary frame.
