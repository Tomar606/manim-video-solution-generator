Medium shot, the camera holding still. The man from the reference images, in his plain black short-sleeve polo shirt with a cream cotton cloth over his left shoulder, kneels at a scribe's low wooden desk in a pillared sandstone court. He steadies a palm leaf while the scribe cuts letters into it, then lifts the leaf and looks up at the camera. He says in Hindi, in a warm, clear, unhurried teacher's voice: "आरंभिक जैन और बौद्ध लेखकों ने कारण अलग बताया, ये शासकों की नीतियों का परिणाम था।" He finishes, closes his mouth and hands the leaf back to the scribe. The scribe works beside him. His is the only voice. The scratch of a stylus and a quiet court. Natural, true-to-life colour; a clean, uncluttered documentary frame.
