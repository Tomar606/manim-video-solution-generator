Low-angle medium shot, the camera holding still. The man from the reference images, in his plain black short-sleeve polo shirt with a cream cotton cloth over his left shoulder, sits up on the elephant's back behind the mahout in the misty sal forest. He ducks under a low branch as the elephant walks on, then looks down at the camera. He says in Hindi, in a warm, clear, unhurried teacher's voice: "उस जमाने में हाथी सबसे बड़ा हथियार था। जिसके पास हाथी, उसकी सेना सबसे आगे।" He finishes, closes his mouth and holds on as the elephant passes the camera. His is the only voice. Heavy footfalls, creaking rope and forest birds. Natural, true-to-life colour; a clean, uncluttered documentary frame.
