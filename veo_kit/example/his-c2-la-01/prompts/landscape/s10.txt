Medium shot from the deck, the camera moving with the boat. The man from the reference images, in his plain black short-sleeve polo shirt with a cream cotton cloth over his left shoulder, stands on the deck of a loaded wooden cargo boat on the wide Ganga at first light. He pushes a long bamboo pole down into the river and walks it along the deck, then looks up at the camera. He says in Hindi, in a warm, clear, unhurried teacher's voice: "चौथा और आखिरी कारण, गंगा और उसकी उपनदियों से आना-जाना सरल और सस्ता था।" He finishes, closes his mouth and leans into the pole again. Bales and clay jars are stacked behind him. His is the only voice. Water lapping against the hull and a distant boat call. Natural, true-to-life colour; a clean, uncluttered documentary frame.
