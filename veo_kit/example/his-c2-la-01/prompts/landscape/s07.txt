Medium shot, the camera holding still. The man from the reference images, in his plain black short-sleeve polo shirt with a cream cotton cloth over his left shoulder, stands inside a small village forge lit by an orange fire, beside the anvil. He works the leather bellows so the coals flare, then takes a finished iron sickle from the smith and turns to the camera. He says in Hindi, in a warm, clear, unhurried teacher's voice: "इससे हथियार और औज़ार बनाना सरल था। सेना भी अपनी, और हथियार भी अपने ही इलाके से।" He finishes, closes his mouth and runs his thumb along the blade. The smith hammers a glowing bar on the anvil beside him. His is the only voice. The ring of the hammer and the roar of the bellows. Natural, true-to-life colour; a clean, uncluttered documentary frame.
