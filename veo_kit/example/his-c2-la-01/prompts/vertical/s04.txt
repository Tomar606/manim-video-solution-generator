Medium shot at his eye level, the camera holding still. The man from the reference images, in his plain black short-sleeve polo shirt with a cream cotton cloth over his left shoulder, stands waist-deep in a dense field of ripe golden rice at low sun, a short hand sickle in his right hand. He gathers a handful of stalks, cuts them, straightens and lifts the cut sheaf, then looks into the camera. He says in Hindi, in a warm, clear, unhurried teacher's voice: "पहला कारण ये है कि मगध क्षेत्र में खेती की उपज खास तौर पर बहुत अच्छी होती थी।" He finishes, closes his mouth and nods once, still holding the sheaf. Two farmers in undyed cotton cut the same row a little behind him. His is the only voice. The rustle of rice stalks and the swish of sickles. Natural, true-to-life colour; a clean, uncluttered tall vertical frame, with him centred, his head near the top and his hands in view in front of his chest.
