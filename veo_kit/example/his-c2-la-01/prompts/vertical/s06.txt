Medium shot, the camera holding still. The man from the reference images, in his plain black short-sleeve polo shirt with a cream cotton cloth over his left shoulder, stands down in a shallow open ore pit of red earth, his forearms dusted red, a pick in his hands. He levers a lump of dark iron ore free with the pick, lifts it and turns it in his hand towards the camera. He says in Hindi, in a warm, clear, unhurried teacher's voice: "अब दूसरा कारण, मगध वाले इलाके में लोहे की खदानें भी बड़ी आसानी से उपलब्ध थीं।" He finishes, closes his mouth and weighs the ore in his palm. Two diggers work the red wall of the pit behind him. His is the only voice. The knock of picks on stone. Natural, true-to-life colour; a clean, uncluttered tall vertical frame, with him centred, his head near the top and his hands in view in front of his chest.
