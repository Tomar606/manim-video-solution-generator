Live-action video footage, filmed with a real camera. Wide shot from high up, the camera holding still, framed tall. A crowded railway platform in India in the late nineteen forties. Steam drifts across the roof beams and thins in the light. Families wait among tin trunks and cloth bundles; a porter walks the length of the platform and a locomotive breathes at the far end. Continuous motion throughout. Muted, natural colour; a tall vertical frame.
