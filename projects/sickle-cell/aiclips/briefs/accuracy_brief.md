**ACCURACY BRIEF — सिकल सेल एनीमिया (NCERT Class 12, Principles of Inheritance)**

**Beat 0 — Normal RBC**
Biconcave disc: circular from above, dumbbell/hourglass in cross-section — dimple on **both** faces, not a bowl or a doughnut with a hole. ~7–8 µm diameter. Uniform red. **No nucleus** (mature mammalian RBC) — never draw a dark central blob and call it a nucleus; the pale centre is thinness, not an organelle. Shown squeezing through capillaries narrower than itself, deforming and springing back.

**Beat 1 — Haemoglobin & gene**
Syllabus simplification: show HbA as 4 subunits — 2 α + 2 β, colour-coded in pairs. Mutation is on the **β chain only**; α chains unchanged. DNA: **GAG → GUG** (write on the coding/sense strand, exactly these three letters; T, not U — this is DNA). Only **one** base changes: the middle A→T. Do not animate a deletion or a shift — it is a point substitution, frame stays intact.

**Beat 2 — Amino acid change**
Position **6** of the β chain, counting from the N-terminus. Glutamic acid (acidic, polar) → **Valine** (non-polar). Change exactly **one** bead in the chain; the other beads stay. Label result **HbS**, normal **HbA**. Superscripts/casing: HbA, HbS — never "HBs" or "HgB".

**Beat 3 — Sickling**
Trigger is **low oxygen tension** — show O₂ level dropping first, then polymerisation. HbS molecules stack into **long straight rigid fibres/rods**, which then distort the cell from inside. Order matters: mutation → HbS → deoxygenation → polymer → shape change. Final shape: **crescent / sickle (हँसिया)** — pointed at both ends, one concave edge, one convex. Not a banana, not a star, not a spiky cell.

**Beat 4 — Pathology**
Sickled cells shown **rigid**, jamming and stacking at a vessel branch/narrowing; blood flow stops behind the block. Also show cells rupturing (haemolysis) → anaemia. Mixed field: sickle cells among normal ones, never 100% sickled.

**Beat 5 — Inheritance**
**Autosomal recessive.** Punnett: HbA HbS × HbA HbS → **HbA HbA : HbA HbS : HbS HbS = 1 : 2 : 1**, i.e. 25% diseased, 50% carrier, 25% normal. Carrier = heterozygous, drawn **normal-looking**, not half-sick. Place gene on an **autosome** — never on X or Y. Both parents' contributions equal; do not make the mother the sole source.

**COMMON ERRORS TO AVOID**
- Nucleus drawn inside a mammalian RBC
- RBC as a flat circle or ring/doughnut with a hole
- Sickle cell drawn as a smooth banana or crescent moon with blunt ends
- U instead of T in the DNA triplet (GAG→GUG)
- Mutation shown on the α chain, or all four chains changed
- Position 5 or 7 instead of 6
- Sickling shown as permanent/unprovoked, with no oxygen trigger
- Carriers drawn with sickled cells or symptoms
- X-linked pedigree symbols or sex chromosomes on screen
- Every cell in the field sickled
