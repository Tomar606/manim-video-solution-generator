[narrator]
Aaj hum quadratic formula step by step derive karenge.
%% Left half: title card "Completing the Square" fades in over a dark midnight gradient; keep the right half empty.
$$ ax^2 + bx + c = 0 $$

[narrator]
Yeh hai general quadratic equation, jahan a, b aur c constants hain aur a zero nahi ho sakta.
%% Left half: highlight coefficients a, b, c one by one in different colours; small note "a ≠ 0" appears below.
$$ ax^2 + bx + c = 0,\quad a \neq 0 $$

[narrator]
Sabse pehle constant term ko right side bhej dete hain, taaki left side pe sirf x wale terms bachein.
%% Left half: animate c sliding across the equals sign and becoming minus c.
$$ ax^2 + bx = -c $$

[narrator]
Ab dono sides ko a se divide karenge, kyunki completing the square ke liye leading coefficient one chahiye.
%% Left half: draw a horizontal division bar under each side, "÷ a" label pulses beside the equation.
$$ x^2 + \frac{b}{a}x = -\frac{c}{a} $$

[narrator]
Yahan trick yeh hai: x ke coefficient ka aadha lo, phir usko square karo.
%% Left half: isolate b/a, animate it halving to b/2a, then a square bracket wraps it to show squaring.
$$ \left(\frac{b}{2a}\right)^2 = \frac{b^2}{4a^2} $$

[narrator]
Is quantity ko dono sides mein add karte hain, taaki equality bilkul balanced rahe.
%% Left half: the new term flies in and attaches to both sides simultaneously; a balance-scale icon tips level.
$$ x^2 + \frac{b}{a}x + \frac{b^2}{4a^2} = -\frac{c}{a} + \frac{b^2}{4a^2} $$

[narrator]
Left side ab ek perfect square ban chuka hai, jo x plus b upon two a ka square hai.
%% Left half: the three left-hand terms collapse together into a single boxed squared bracket with a glow.
$$ \left(x + \frac{b}{2a}\right)^2 = \frac{b^2}{4a^2} - \frac{c}{a} $$

[narrator]
Right side ko ek common denominator four a square pe laate hain aur simplify karte hain.
%% Left half: show c/a being multiplied by 4a/4a, then both fractions merging into one.
$$ \left(x + \frac{b}{2a}\right)^2 = \frac{b^2 - 4ac}{4a^2} $$

[narrator]
Dono sides ka square root lete hain, aur yaad rakhna, root ke saath plus minus zaroori hai.
%% Left half: radical symbol sweeps over both sides; the ± sign flashes to draw attention.
$$ x + \frac{b}{2a} = \pm\frac{\sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a} $$

[narrator]
Last step, b upon two a ko left side se hata kar right side pe shift kar dete hain.
%% Left half: b/2a slides across the equals sign, turning negative and joining the fraction.
$$ x = -\frac{b}{2a} \pm \frac{\sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a} $$

[narrator]
Dono terms ka denominator same hai, toh inhe milakar hamara famous quadratic formula ban jaata hai.
%% Left half: the two fractions merge into one; final formula scales up and gets a bright border.
$$ x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a} $$

[narrator]
Root ke andar wala part discriminant kehlata hai, aur wahi decide karta hai roots real hain ya nahi.
%% Left half: highlight b²−4ac in gold; three short labels appear beside it — positive, zero, negative.
$$ D = b^2 - 4ac $$
