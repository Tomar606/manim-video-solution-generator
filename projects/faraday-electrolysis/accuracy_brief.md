# ACCURACY BRIEF — फैराडे के विद्युत्-अपघटन के नियम (MP Board Class 12)

## Symbols on screen — exact forms

- **W** (mass liberated), **Q** (quantity of electricity, coulomb), **i** (current, ampere — lowercase italic *i*, NOT *I*, NOT *A*), **t** (time, seconds), **Z** (electrochemical equivalent, capital Z), **E** (chemical equivalent, capital E).
- **∝** is the proportionality sign — a curled alpha-like glyph, NOT the Greek letter α and NOT "=". Never render it as ∞.
- Subscripts are **W₁, W₂, E₁, E₂** — subscript, never superscript, never W1/W2 inline.
- **Q = i × t** — order matters; do not write t × i or Q = i/t.
- **W = Z i t** — no multiplication dots between Z, i, t on screen (script shows juxtaposition). Z comes first.
- Second law ratio is **W₁ / W₂ = E₁ / E₂** — same subscript order both sides. Flipping one side (W₁/W₂ = E₂/E₁) is wrong.

## Build order (the derivation must appear in this sequence)

Beat 1: `W ∝ Q` → `Q = i × t` → `W ∝ i × t` → `W = Z i t`. The constant Z appears **only at the last step**, at the moment narration says "समानुपात हटाने पर". Do not pre-place Z.
Beat 2: `W ∝ E` → `W₁ ∝ E₁` and `W₂ ∝ E₂` (these two together) → `W₁ / W₂ = E₁ / E₂`.

## If an electrolysis cell is drawn (optional visual)

- Two electrodes dipped in liquid, connected to a **battery** — cell symbol: long thin line = positive, short thick line = negative.
- **Anode = positive terminal, cathode = negative** in an electrolytic cell (opposite of a galvanic cell — do not copy a galvanic diagram).
- Current arrows in the external wire flow from + to −. Cations (+) move toward the cathode, anions (−) toward the anode. Arrows in the solution must point in **opposite** directions for the two ion types.
- Circuit must be closed and include the ammeter/current path; a battery with a broken wire while "current flows" is a visible error.
- Electrodes must not touch each other or the beaker bottom.

## Second-law staging (the script explicitly flags the confusion)

Show **two or more different electrolytic cells in series** — same current, same time, different electrolytes. Series wiring is the visual point: one loop through both cells. Two cells side by side on separate batteries contradicts "समान मात्रा में विद्युत्".

## Must never appear

- No digits or units spoken/labelled where the script gives none — no "96500 C", no Faraday constant F; the script never introduces it.
- No "2023 या 2025" written as text over beat 0 unless the answer card does it; narration handles it.
- No arrows or highlight on Q in the second law — second law's relationship is W↔E, with Q constant.
- No AC source, no bulb, no switch drawn open.

## COMMON ERRORS TO AVOID

1. Drawing a **galvanic/voltaic cell** (anode negative) instead of an electrolytic cell.
2. Writing **I** (capital) instead of **i**, or **W = Zit** with Z looking like the number 2.
3. Rendering **∝** as **α**, **∞**, or **=**.
4. Both ion arrows in the electrolyte pointing the same way.
5. Inverting one side of **W₁/W₂ = E₁/E₂**.
6. Introducing Z or the constant too early in the beat-1 build.
7. Cells drawn in parallel rather than series for the second law.
8. Bubbles shown at only one electrode when the visual implies both are active.
