## Screen rules carried in

- Screen text ORGANISES; it never repeats the caption underneath it.
- Checklist items and map labels persist once shown; nothing fades to make room.
- Counted lists use digits on screen: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 — never dashes or rules.
- No em-dashes or en-dashes anywhere on screen.
- Sound sits on the teaching beat — an item resolving — never on a caption.
- Classical map conventions; the house palette comes second.

## Presenter placement

Measured off the delivered science videos and cross-checked against the POC's
reel.json, which used the same number independently:

- width about 505-520px of 1080, centred at x=50%, anchored to the bottom edge
- head top lands around y=860, i.e. 45% down
- everything that teaches lives ABOVE that line, in the top 60% of the frame
- `avatar_cover_gate` is what enforces it: it diffs the background against the
  final and fails on ink that exists in one and not the other. It caught a list
  item printed across his forehead on a build whose other four gates passed.

## Veo b-roll — all generated fresh, nothing reused

Every clip is a SCENE of the thing being taught. No presenter, no teacher figure,
no stand-in for the narrator: the presenter is the HeyGen plate and appears only
there. And no empty rooms, empty streets or objects on a desk — the earlier batch
tried an empty lane for Razakar violence and an empty chamber for Pakistan
arguing at the UN, and they teach nothing. Every prompt names people, what they
are doing, and India.

Prompt rules, each from a generation that came back wrong:

- open "Live-action video footage, filmed with a real camera" — without it Flow
  reads the prompt as describing a photograph and returns a still
- name INDIA and the period clothing in every prompt — the first batch said
  "ordinary townspeople" and came back European, flat caps and cobbled streets
- state what MOVES; every shot has motion named, never "wide static shot"
- no text, no lettering, no signage in any language — Devanagari is composited
- no presenter, teacher, narrator or person addressing the camera
- no measurement, percentage or zone name anywhere in the prompt
- tall frame

Each clip is delogoed (Flow burns its sparkle into every generation), QC'd, and
fitted to its window by slow, loop or hold — never reversed.
