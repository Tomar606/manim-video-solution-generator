Medium wide shot, the camera drifting slowly sideways, in the warm living room of a middle-class Indian home. Three generations of one family sit close together on a sofa for a family photograph: a grandfather and grandmother in the centre, their son and daughter-in-law on either side, and a boy and a girl of school age sitting on the floor in front. The family resemblance is strong: the same wavy black hair and the same broad smile run from the grandparents to the parents to the children. The grandfather puts an arm around his grandson and everyone laughs together. Soft evening light from a lamp; a framed family portrait hangs on the wall behind them. The only sound is gentle laughter and room ambience. Natural, true-to-life colour; a clean, uncluttered documentary frame.
