* Everything is drawn by hand, without instruments: circles are visibly out of round, pentagons and boxes are lopsided with unequal sides, "parallel" lines are not parallel and drift together or apart, and repeated shapes down a row are all slightly different sizes.
* Straight lines are drawn straight-in-feel but are never dead level and often overshoot slightly at corners and junctions.
