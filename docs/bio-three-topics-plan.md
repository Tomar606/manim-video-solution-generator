# BIO-C1-LA-01 · BIO-C1-LA-02 · BIO-C5-LA-03 — visual plan

Derived from the three scripts in `inbox/bio_scripts/`, read line by line. Counts
below distinguish **already built** (in `~/Desktop/Diagrams/biology/`) from **new**.

## The finding that changes the schedule

**LA-01 and LA-02 are the same anatomy seen twice.** The बीजाण्ड of LA-01 *is*
the गुरुबीजाणुधानी of LA-02; the भ्रूणकोष of LA-01 *is* what LA-02's functional
megaspore becomes. One correct anatropous-ovule figure — integuments, micropyle,
nucellus, chalaza, embryo sac with its seven cells — serves both videos, and the
7-cells/8-nuclei interior is reused wholesale.

Build the ovule once, properly, and LA-02's megaspore column is a re-dress of it.
Building them separately is the expensive mistake here.

## 1. BIO-C1-LA-01 — प्रारूपिक आवृतबीजी बीजाण्ड (6 marks, 2 parts)

**Nothing built. I previously recommended skipping this one and that was wrong.**
I argued that a question asking for *स्पष्ट एवं साफ-सुथरा नामांकित चित्र* wants a
static line drawing, so animation adds nothing. But the animation here is not
decoration — it is **the drawing order**. The student watches the figure assembled
in exactly the sequence they must reproduce it, one named part at a time. That is
the most useful thing a 6-mark diagram question can be given.

### The figure must be ANATROPOUS, and that is where these drawings go wrong

The script opens with *"आवृतबीजियों में एनाट्रॉपस बीजाण्ड सबसे सामान्य प्रकार का
बीजाण्ड होता है"*. Anatropous means the body is inverted through 180°, so:

- the **micropyle ends up beside the hilum**, not opposite it
- the **funicle runs along the flank and fuses to the body as the raphe**
- the **chalaza is at the far end from the micropyle**, which is now the end away
  from the stalk

An orthotropous drawing (straight, micropyle at the top away from the stalk) is
the standard error and a chemistry-teacher-spots-it mistake.

| a03 | 1 | अध्यावरण — one or two, usually two | two distinct layers, and they must not close over the micropyle |

a02–a06 are **one figure growing**, not six pictures. Each new part appears on the
same ovule and its label stays. Nothing that has been named ever leaves the screen.

### Veo — one optional shot
A flower's ovary being opened to reveal ovules, 3–4 s, as the opener over a01.
No text, no labels, no numbers — it cannot carry any of those. Cheaper as stock
footage than through the Flow bridge; take it only if the opener feels flat.

### Photographs — 2
- **Ovule L.S. photomicrograph** — right at the start, to make the schematic
  credible. **Establishing shot only, never labelled part-by-part**: a micrograph
  does not tell you which end is chalazal with enough confidence to caption it,
  and the electrolysis photo already taught us what happens when we try.
- **Pistil cut open showing ovules on the placenta** — pairs with a01.

## 2. BIO-C1-LA-02 — लघु- व गुरुबीजाणुजनन (3 parts) — **7 clips already built**

The script makes eight numbered points and all eight are covered by the existing
`BIO-C1-LA-02_a01..a07`. What is worth **upgrading**, not rebuilding:

| clip | as built | upgrade |
|---|---|---|
| a02 location | small 4-lobe anther + ovule | proper **dithecous, quadrangular anther T.S.** — the script says *चतुष्कोणीय* and *चारों कोणों पर एक-एक लघुबीजाणुधानी*; the count of four is the point |
| a03 wall-layers | a text list | **concentric section of one microsporangium**, the four layers drawn outside-in — एपिडर्मिस → एण्डोथीसियम → मध्य परत → टैपीटम. The script: *"क्रम उल्टा हुआ तो number नहीं मिलेंगे."* A list does not teach an order; rings do |
| a06 four-vs-one | micro tetrad drawn 2×2 | microspore tetrad is **tetrahedral**, megaspore tetrad is **linear** — that contrast is itself examinable, and drawing both as grids loses it |
| — | | **new a08: meiosis count** — MMC (2n) → 4; run both columns side by side so *many MMCs vs exactly one* lands visually |

### Veo — none
The organic element is the meiotic division, but every mark here is a count: four
corners, four wall layers, four microspores, one of four megaspores. A generated
clip cannot be held to a number.

### Photographs — 3
- **Anther T.S. micrograph** — the classic four-lobed section; excellent under

- **Pollen grains under the microscope** — under *परागकण* in the final point
- **Ovule L.S.** — the same photograph as LA-01, reused

## 3. BIO-C5-LA-03 — वाटसन-क्रिक द्विकुण्डली (3 parts) — **8 clips already built**

All nine of the script's points are covered by `BIO-C5-LA-03_a01..a08`, and this
one is in the best shape of the three. Two additions worth making:

| | |
|---|---|
| **purine vs pyrimidine** | Point (iv) says *"प्रत्येक क्षारक युग्म में एक प्यूरीन और एक पिरीमिडीन"*. Draw the **double ring against the single ring** — that is why the width is constant, which is point (v). Currently it is a line of text under a04; as a shape it explains the next point instead of just stating this one |
| **the ladder ⇄ helix morph, once more at the end** | a03 twists the flat ladder into the helix. Running it **backwards** in part 3 — helix untwisting to the 2D ladder — hands the student the diagram they must actually draw. Free in Manim, impossible in Veo |

### Veo — one optional shot
A chromosome opening out to reveal DNA, 3–4 s, as the hook before any
measurement. No text and no numbers — this question is nine measurements, and
none of them can be trusted to a generated clip.

### Photographs — 1, with a licensing caveat
- **A Watson–Crick model photograph** is justified because the script names them.
  The famous 1953 Barrington Brown photograph is **not** free to use; so is Photo
  51 in most jurisdictions. Use `tools/fetch_photos.py`, which filters Commons to
  licences permitting commercial use, and take a modern B-DNA model render if no
  clean historical image comes back.

## Where Veo earns its place, honestly

Across all three: **the opening three seconds, and nothing else.** Every mark in
these questions is a count, a name, an order or a measurement — four wall layers,
one functional megaspore of four, 2 nm, 3.4 nm, ten base pairs, seven cells and
eight nuclei. A generated clip cannot set Devanagari, cannot be held to a number,
and will not draw the same apparatus twice. It can open a reel handsomely; it
cannot teach any part of these three answers.
