[narrator]
Yahan ek positive charge rakha hua hai. Iske chaaro taraf Electric Field har direction mein bahar ki taraf ja rahi hai.
%% dur: 10 — A positive charge sends its Electric Field outward in every direction

[narrator]
Ye lines electrons nahi hain. Ye sirf field ki direction aur strength ko represent karti hain.
%% dur: 6 — These lines aren't electrons — they show the field's direction & strength

[narrator]
Is charge se total kitni Electric Field bahar nikal rahi hai? Kya hum har field line ko count kar sakte hain?
%% dur: 6 — How much field escapes in total? Can we count every line?

[narrator]
Nahi. Isi problem ko solve karne ke liye Gauss's Law use kiya jata hai.
%% dur: 4 — We can't. So we use Gauss's Law.

[narrator]
Charge ke around ek imaginary closed surface bana dete hain. Is surface ko Gaussian Surface kehte hain.
%% dur: 8 — Draw an imaginary closed surface around the charge — a Gaussian Surface

[narrator]
Gaussian Surface koi real object nahi hota. Ye sirf calculation ko easy banane ke liye ek imaginary closed surface hota hai.
%% dur: 10 — It's imaginary — only to make the calculation easy

[narrator]
Jitni Electric Field is closed surface ko cross karti hai, uski total quantity ko hum Electric Flux kehte hain.
%% dur: 9 — Total field crossing the closed surface = Electric Flux

[narrator]
Simple language mein — Electric Flux matlab kisi closed surface se guzarne wali total Electric Field.
%% dur: 7 — Electric Flux = Total electric field crossing a closed surface

[narrator]
Surface ke har chhote area element dA ka ek Area Vector hota hai.
%% dur: 7 — Every area element dA has an Area Vector

[narrator]
Aur closed surface ke liye Area Vector hamesha surface ke bahar ki taraf hota hai.
%% dur: 7 — For a closed surface, the Area Vector always points outward

[narrator]
Electric Flux nikalne ke liye hum Electric Field aur Area Vector ka dot product lete hain.
%% dur: 6 — Electric Flux uses the dot product of E and the Area Vector

[narrator]
Sirf Electric Field ka woh component count hota hai jo Area Vector ki direction mein ho.
%% dur: 6 — Only the field component along the Area Vector counts

[narrator]
Kisi bhi closed surface se nikalne wala total Electric Flux, us surface ke andar enclosed charge ke proportional hota hai.
%% dur: 5 — Total flux is proportional to the charge enclosed

[narrator]
Yehi hai Gauss's Law.
%% dur: 3 — This is Gauss's Law

[narrator]
Ab iska derivation dekhte hain agle part mein.
%% dur: 4 — Next: the derivation
