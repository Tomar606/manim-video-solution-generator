**Frame Style Specification**

**Canvas & background.** Pure white (#FFFFFF), no gradient or texture. Each frame is a self-contained card with a thin light-grey hairline border. Generous white margins; content floats, never bleeds.

**Furniture (fixed, every frame).**
- Top-left: dark navy filled circle (~7% of frame width) with a white bold numeral — the step number.
- Top-centre/right of that circle: the title in heavy uppercase sans, navy (#12305E), letter-spaced slightly. Title never wraps past two words per line.
- Bottom: caption box — white fill, 1.5px dark rounded-rect stroke, full content width, 1–2 lines of centred dark-navy sentence-case text at ~40% of title size.

**Palette with roles.** Navy = structure, titles, numbering, neutral labels. Green (#1B7A3D) = the favourable/high/positive state. Red (#D0202A) = the opposing/low/negative state. Purple = a third parallel factor only when three are needed. Mid-blue = annotation accents and arrows ONLY. Amber/gold outline = the single "key condition" callout. Grey = inert scaffolding and vessel outlines. Never use a colour decoratively; each appearance must carry its role.

**Subject colour is never set by this palette.** The palette above governs annotation furniture — labels, arrows, pills, headers, caption box. The subject itself is always rendered in its true, scientifically correct colour (red blood cells are red, chlorophyll green, venous blood dark red). If the subject's real colour clashes with a role colour, keep the subject correct and move the annotation colour.

**Typography.** One geometric/humanist sans throughout, bold for labels, semibold for captions. Formulas set in serif italic maths with coloured variables matching their explanation boxes.

**Labels.** Short 1–3 word phrases inside white rounded-rect boxes with thin outlines, placed in the outer margin and attached to the diagram by a straight black arrow with a solid triangular head. Arrows are horizontal or vertical, never curved except when linking formula terms downward.

**Comparisons.** Split the frame with a faint vertical rule; identical diagrams left and right, each capped by a green or red header pill and closed by a matching coloured conclusion box at the bottom.

**Realism.** Photorealistic vector-rendered object (soft shading, highlights, cast-free) against white; particle-level detail shown as small spheres in a colour that contrasts with the subject (blue on a red subject, and vice versa), plus a circular magnifier inset with a leader line for zoom-in. Small realistic gauge/meter dials indicate state, labelled HIGH/LOW in colour.

**Final frame.** Two-column recap, formula restated in a tinted box, navy banner with title + checkmark, then a bold call-out line mixing navy and red.
