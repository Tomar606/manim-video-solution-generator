## 1. The direction

**"दो स्याही" — a two-ink printed poster, and one line that falls and rises.**

This answer is a tug of war between two sides: **राजा** and **जनता**. Power goes
to the people in 1990, is snatched back in 2002, is won again in 2006, and the
crown is gone in 2008. A student who sees that shape can rebuild all six points
from it.

So the whole video is printed in two inks on cream paper, and each ink means
one side:

- **लाल स्याही (crimson) = राजा, राजशाही**
- **नीली स्याही (deep blue) = जनता, लोकतंत्र**

Every picture, heading and stamp is in the ink of whoever holds power in that
sentence. When the king shuts parliament the page goes red. When the crowd
fills the street it goes blue. The colour is doing the teaching.

**Why this helps the student:** he himself says *"ये points साल के क्रम में चलते
हैं… साल याद रहे तो points अपने आप याद रहेंगे"*. The video takes him at his word
and turns the years into one picture.

## 2. The spine: "लोकतंत्र की राह"

One line across the top of the page, with the years on it. It is low and red
while the king rules, and climbs and turns blue when the people win.

```
 लोकतंत्र ▲                                    ●━━━━━━━●
          │            ●╌╌╮                   ╱ 2008    2015
          │           ╱    ╲                 ╱
          │          ╱ 1990 ╲               ● 2006
 राजा का  │━━━━━━━━━●        ╲             ╱
 शासन     │  पृष्ठभूमि          ●━━━━━━━━━━━●
          └──────────────────── 2002 ─────────────────▶
              red            red again        blue
```

- It first appears when he says "1990 से 2015 तक": two named ends and unnamed
  dots between. Each dot gets its year only when he reaches it (rule 8).
- It sits as a thin strip at the top of almost every scene, the current point
  glowing, so the student always knows where he is in the answer.
- **Part 1 ends with the line on the floor at 2002** and a dotted "?" ahead.
  **Part 2 ends with it at the top.** **Part 3 redraws the whole line** point by
  point for the recap, which is exactly what he is saying there.
- The name "लोकतंत्र की राह" is his own phrase (part 1, 1:15); it is printed on
  the strip only from that moment.

The height of the line is a sketch of who holds power, not a measurement. It
has no scale and no numbers on that axis.

## 4. Where the presenter is — three states, each with a meaning

```
 EXPLAINING              TO YOU                  THE LINE
 (most scenes)           (hook, exam lines)      (roadmap, the date, recap)
┌──────────────┐       ┌──────────────┐        ┌──────────────┐
│ ─●──○──○──○─ │ line  │ ─●──●──○──○─ │        │              │
│ ┌──────────┐ │       │              │        │   the line,  │
│ │ picture  │ │       │   HEADING    │        │   full page  │
│ │ printed  │ │       │   in big     │        │              │
│ │ in 1 ink │ │       │   type       │        │   ●──╮       │
│ └──────────┘ │       │              │        │      ╰──●    │
│  HEADING     │       │    ┌────┐    │        │              │
│      ┌───┐   │       │    │ 😀 │    │        │              │
│      │😀 │   │       │   /│big │\   │        │ ┌──┐         │
│      │   │   │       │    │    │    │        │ │😀│ small   │
│ [ caption ]  │       │ [ caption ]  │        │ [ caption ]  │
└──────────────┘       └──────────────┘        └──────────────┘
 cut-out, lower 45%,     cut-out, large,         hidden, or small
 right of centre         centre                  bottom-left on the recap
```

- **Explaining** — picture above, him below. His face can never be covered,
  because pictures and text live in the top half and he lives in the bottom.
- **To you** — he comes forward, large and centred, on a plain page. Used for
  the hook, each point's heading, "इसे ध्यान से सुनना", and the endings.
- **The line** — the line takes the page. He is hidden for the roadmap (8 s)
  and for the date 28 मई 2008, and small in a corner through the 40 s recap.

He changes state only on a cut, with a 0.5 s glide (rule 10). He is a paper
cut-out: a thin white edge and a soft shadow, so he sits ON the poster.

Each is printed in one ink, so it reads as an illustration and never as a news

## 7. How it differs from the two before it

- **Colour carries meaning.** Two inks, two sides. Neither earlier video did this.
- **Footage is printed, not shown.** Framed in the top half and inked, where
  both earlier videos ran colour footage full-bleed.
- **The spine is a chart, not a place.** Magadha returned to a map, GEO to a
  globe; this returns to a line.
- **He is a paper cut-out on a page**, not a figure in a scene, a circle or a window.
- **Paper sounds**, not a tanpura or a synth pad.

## 9. How this build is made faster than the last one

The last video took 2 h 25 min of working time, plus 4 h 16 min lost when the
session died overnight, plus 13 wasted Veo generations.
