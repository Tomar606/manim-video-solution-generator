## 1. The direction

**"इमारत का नक़्शा" — the blueprint.**

His opening line is the whole answer in one picture: *"जैसे एक मज़बूत घर चार खंभों
पर टिका होता है, वैसे ही मानव विकास भी चार खंभों पर टिका है."* NCERT draws the same
thing (Fig. 3.1, four pillars under a roof). So the video is an **architect's drawing
of that building**, drawn in white lines on blueprint blue, one pillar at a time:

- the **roof** is मानव विकास, and it is there from the first sentence;
- each **pillar** gets a number on its capital and a row in the drawing's
  **legend** (① समता …), the way an architect keys a drawing;
- each pillar's **one-line meaning** is hand-lettered beside its name, because his
  exam tip is "हर नाम के साथ एक लाइन का मतलब";
- the **recap** is the finished drawing: four pillars, four legend rows, name +
  meaning, every one lit.

**Why this helps the student:** the answer is a list of four names plus a line each.
A building that cannot stand on three pillars makes "four" stick, the legend IS the
answer in writing order, and "name + one line" is drawn as the format of every row.

**Rough sketch:** `board/sheet.png` — three frames (the four names at 0:33, the two axes at 1:11, the recap at 2:10) with a real frame of him and a stand-in background (an old GEO clip, blurred and toned). The caption in the third frame overflows only because the sketch puts the whole sentence on one line; real captions break into short lines.
