## 1. The direction

**"गोल मेज़" — the round table.**

His own sentence is the whole answer in one picture: *"जो देश पहले अलग-अलग अपनी
समस्याओं से जूझ रहे थे, उन्हें साथ बैठकर बात करने की एक जगह मिली."* SAARC **is**
a table. So the whole video happens on one large round conference table, seen from
straight above:

- the **परिचय** is who sits at it (a map inlaid into the wood, eight named pins);
- each of the **five points** is a card laid on the table, in order;
- the **fifth point** (रुकावटें) is the table cracking;
- the **निष्कर्ष** is the table still standing: *"आज भी एक अहम मंच"*.

**Why this helps the student:** the answer has a fixed shape — परिचय, five points,
निष्कर्ष — and he says so twice. Five cards on one table, in the order he laid them,
is a picture a student can rebuild in the exam hall.

## 2. The spine: the table and its five cards

```

         ▣                                  ▣      ▣ = eight brass seat plates
       ▣     ┌────────────────────────┐       ▣        (unnamed: "देश")
             │   round maroon disc =  │
       ▣     │   the picture window   │       ▣    pictures (Veo) appear INSIDE
             │   (Veo clip, or ours)  │            the disc, as through glass
         ▣   └────────────────────────┘     ▣
              [1] [2] [3] [4] [5]                  five card slots under the disc
```

- The table appears when he says **"एक मंच पर लाकर"** (P1, 1:05). Before that the disc
  holds the hook pictures and the map.
- Each point's card is **laid in its slot when he names it**, and stays for the rest
  of the video, so the student always knows where they are in the answer.
- Between points the table makes a **quarter-turn** (the seat plates move round one place).
- The seat plates carry **no country names**: the table stands for "the member
  countries", never for eight founders in 1985 (Afghanistan joined in 2007; see brief).

## 7. How it differs from the three before it

- **The spine is an object, not a place or a chart:** a table (Magadha a map, GEO a
  globe, POL-02 a line).
- **Pictures are round, in colour, set into the table** — not full-bleed (Magadha,
  GEO) and not one-ink prints in a square frame (POL-02).
- **The map is inlaid wood**, not a relief documentary map or a night globe.
- **Warm wood, brass and leather**, not paper, night or earth tones.
- **Marimba and wood** sounds; **iris and table-turn** transitions; **brass-plaque** captions.
