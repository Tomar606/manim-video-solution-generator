## POL-C3-LA-05 — "गोल मेज़" (the round table) (2026-10-08)

Two reels: 1:52, 2:03. Question: the contribution of the SAARC countries to the
development of South Asia (MP Board 2024).

- **Spine:** one round conference table seen from above. Its maroon leather disc is
  the picture window (Veo clips play ROUND, cut by the under layer, with an iris on
  every picture change); eight brass seat plates arrive round the ring when "the
  countries sit together" and the table makes a quarter-turn at every new point; a
  strip at the top fills परिचय · five point cards (number + icon) · निष्कर्ष. Point 5
  cracks the table in coral; the निष्कर्ष heals it and lights the brass ring.
- **Palette / type:** walnut wood (drawn), brass #d9a841, cream cards #f6eedc, ink,
  maroon leather #5c1f24, coral #e2553f only for the challenges, teal #2f7d6d for
  growth. Baloo 2 (headings, cards), Hind Bold (captions, body), Yatra One (numbers).
- **Pictures:** Veo in full colour inside the round disc, never full-bleed.
- **Presenter, four states:** large centre under a shrunk disc = talking to you;
  medium bottom-centre under the table = explaining; hidden = the map or the cracked
  table; inside a brass-rimmed round "seat" bottom-right = the recap.
- **Captions:** one line on a brass plaque, dark engraved Hind, the spoken word maroon.
- **Devices:** 100 figures with 22 lit, rows of five; an inlaid-wood map (Natural Earth
  10m land + relief, NO political line) with eight brass pins named as he says them

  goods cross; four icon tiles; arrows round the ring; three named cracks; "should

  picture back in the disc; the three-tray skeleton with ticks.
- **Sound:** a harmonium-like reed drone on one clock, wooden knocks, a card sliding

  chime per point, a dry crack, an iris whoosh; nothing on a word.
- **Veo:** 6 clips of manner (a crowded street from above, a summit table from above,

  older doctor guiding a younger one). No flag, no name card, no lettering.
