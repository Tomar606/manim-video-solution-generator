## 1. The direction

**"रैंच का नक्शा" (the ranch plan), seen from above.**

The answer is about **land being divided and managed**: fences, parcels, grass, how many
animals a parcel can carry. That is a plan, so the whole video happens on **one ranch drawn
as a plan, seen straight down**, with white fence lines over real grass footage:

- **The परिभाषा** is a contrast drawn on that ground: the nomad's wandering dotted track vs
  one fenced square that stays put.
- **Feature 1, स्थायी रैंच:** the big fence is drawn, split into six numbered भूखंड; the
  flock grazes parcel 1 brown, walks through the gate to parcel 2, and parcel 1 grows
  green again. This is the time-lapse a student can redraw in the exam.
- **Feature 2, वहन क्षमता:** one parcel. Too many sheep turn it brown (बंजर); the right
  number keeps it green. **जितनी घास, उतने ही पशु.**
- **Feature 3, विशिष्ट गतिविधि:** the plan becomes four ranches, one kind of animal in
  each; then a chain runs off the plan's edge: मांस · खाल · ऊन → प्रसंस्करण → डिब्बाबंदी → निर्यात.

**Why this helps the student:** each feature has a picture you can draw in ten seconds:
a fence, a parcel and its grass, one animal per ranch. The recap rebuilds the plan's
**कुंजी** (map legend) with those three symbols: the legend **is** the answer.


- A **four-segment strip** at the top of almost every scene (परिभाषा · 1 · 2 · 3); the
  current one glows lime, finished ones turn white. A small lime eyebrow names it.
- The full **कुंजी** opens when he first names the three features (P1 1:07), comes back
  at the start of Part 2 with परिभाषा and 1 ticked, and closes the video with all ticked.
- Part 1 ends on a **cliffhanger**: the कुंजी with 1 ticked and 2, 3 showing "?" and
  **Part 2 →**; a "3 नंबर" meter one third full.
