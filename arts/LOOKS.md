## GEO-C2-LA-01 — "पृथ्वी, रात में" (the Earth at night) (2026-10-07)

Three reels: 2:22, 1:48, 2:29. Question: factors affecting the distribution and
density of world population.

- **Spine:** a night-lights globe (NASA Earth at Night 2012 on a d3 orthographic
  globe) that the video keeps returning to, and a four-part ring round the
  presenter that fills one arc per factor across all three parts and opens into a
  full-screen answer wheel in the recap.
- **Palette / type:** deep navy, amber #ffc857, cyan #5fe1ff, one warning
  red-orange #f0704e (unrest only); Anek Devanagari; satellite-HUD frames and ticks.
- **Presenter, four states, each with a meaning:** full-frame = talking to you;
  portrait window bottom-left (head, chest, hands) = explaining over footage;
  circle = hook on a split screen, the ring, quick comparison shots, progress;
  hidden = the map has the screen. Every change is a 0.5 s glide centred on the cut.
- **Captions:** one line at the bottom, the spoken word lit cyan.
- **Devices:** split screen with him on the seam (crowd / desert; farmland / city);
  population meter; dots pulled IN (economy) and driven OUT (unrest, on a schematic
  region, never a real place); pins with names on the globe; three regions as soft
  generalised shapes with no political border anywhere; a flat night map for the
  three-region recap; the torn exam-sheet question card from the PCB videos.
- **Sound:** a slow synth pad on one clock through all three parts, pings, ticks,
  whooshes, chimes; nothing allowed on a word.
- **Veo:** 18 clips of manner only (market, desert, river, plain, mountains, ice,
  soil, factories, city, mine, rail yard, pilgrims, town, people walking away,

- **Code:** `work/geo-c2-la-01/design/`, build in `tools/reel/`, snapshot in

## POL-C3-LA-02 — "दो स्याही" (two inks) (2026-10-08)

Three reels: 2:17, 2:15, 2:01. Question: how democracy was established in Nepal
(MP Board 2025).

- **Spine:** one line, "लोकतंत्र की राह", across the years 1990 · 2002 · 2006 · 2008 ·
  2015: low and red while the king rules, high and blue when the people win. A
  strip at the top of almost every scene, full-page for the roadmap, the fall at
  2002 and the recap, where it redraws itself point by point with six numbered
  rows under it. A year dot gets its number only when he says that year.
- **Palette / type:** cream paper #f2ead9, ink black, crimson #c8102e = राजा /
  राजशाही, deep blue #12357a = जनता / लोकतंत्र; the page itself tints pink or pale
  blue with whoever holds power. Khand (headings, years), Mukta (body, captions),
  Tiro Devanagari Hindi (the exam lines).
- **Pictures:** Veo clips PRINTED IN ONE INK (grey ramped from the ink to the
  paper) inside a frame in the upper half; a paper shutter slides off the frame.
  Never full-bleed, never in colour.
- **Presenter, three states:** a paper cut-out (white edge, soft shadow) large
  and centred = talking to you; smaller, lower right under the picture =
  explaining; hidden = the line or the date has the page. Small bottom-left
  through the recap. 0.5 s glide on the cut.
- **Captions:** one line on a torn cream strip, the spoken word underlined with
  a crimson marker stroke.
- **Devices:** poster headings stamped on; pinned "यानी" slips; rubber stamps
  (संसद भंग, struck out and restamped संसद बहाल); six pips; the three-slot answer
  skeleton; fill-in tiles (three groups, four agreements); a forked road for the
  Maoists; the full-page date 28 मई 2008 under a 240-वर्ष bar; type-only name
  plates for every real person; a relief-and-rivers map with NO political line,
  three arrows from भारत to नेपाल; the house exam-sheet question card.
- **Sound:** paper slides, stamp thuds, pencil scratch while the line draws, a low
  print-room bed on one clock through all three parts; nothing on a word.
- **Veo:** 10 clips of manner only (empty throne, valley town, crowd in a square,
  a hand signing, hall doors closing, a march from above, doors opening, lamps at
  dusk, a hand writing before an assembly, a paper into a box). No flag, no sign,
  no face of a real person.
- **Code:** `work/pol-c3-la-02/design/` (kit.mjs + part1-3.mjs, captions.json,

Two reels: 1:59, 2:10. Question: three features of commercial livestock rearing
(वाणिज्य पशुधन पालन, MP Board 2024, 3 अंक).

- **Spine:** one ranch drawn as a land plan, chalk fence lines and posts over real grass
  seen straight down (Veo V4). The fence draws, splits into six numbered भूखंड, the flock
  grazes parcel 1 to straw, walks through the gate to parcel 2, parcel 1 grows back. Its

- **Palette / type:** field greens (page `#21402c`), chalk `#f4f1e8`; lime `#c6f04a` =
  घास / enough / the current point, coral `#ff6b4a` = ज़्यादा / बंजर / the mistake.

- **Presenter, four states:** large centre over the clip blurred = talking to you;

- **Captions:** one line of white Hind with a dark outline; the spoken word dark on a lime

- **Devices:** the nomad as a dotted wandering track vs a fenced square that stays;

- **Sound:** wind over grass on one clock through both parts; wire twang per fence line,

## GEO-C3-SA-01 — "इमारत का नक़्शा" (the blueprint) (2026-10-09)

One reel, 2:26. Question: name the four components of human development (MP Board 2024, 3 अंक).

- **Spine:** an architect's drawing of a building on four pillars (NCERT draws the same figure),
  roof titled मानव विकास, with a numbered key under it: one row per pillar, the name in yellow
  and its one-line meaning hand-lettered. Full size for the reveal, the four names and the recap;
  a small copy top-left as the progress device ("घटक n / 4"), the pillar being taught glowing.
- **Palette / type:** footage toned to blueprint navy (#081e3e → #1e4473), lightly blurred, under a faint
  white grid; white technical lines; yellow #ffd54a = the four names only; red #ff6a4d = a wrong
  turn only (the tangle, फसल ✗ मशीन ✗). Baloo 2 ExtraBold (names, headings), Kalam (meanings and
  notes, never digits), Hind Bold (captions).
- **Pictures:** a clip that is the sentence's claim is PINNED sharp and in colour in the upper half,
  in a white mount with corner marks and dimension ticks, over its own toned copy
  (`edit_from_scenes.py` shot `pin`); the hook is the colour clip full frame.
- **Presenter, four states:** large centre = talking to you (hook, each pillar's heading, "ध्यान से
  सुनना", "3 नंबर पक्के", the end); bottom centre = explaining; hidden = the question card; small =
  the recap. A pale drafting-lamp pool behind his shoulders. 0.5 s glide on the cut.
- **Captions:** one line in a navy title-block strip with a white hairline; words to come pale,
  the spoken word white with a pen-stroke underline.
- **Devices:** the name + one-line template (नाम + एक लाइन का मतलब = पूरा answer); three doors and
  five different people with equal arrows (सबको बराबर); three generations each getting the same
  box; NEW: the two axes, समता ↔ across today's people and सतत पोषणीयता ↕ down the generations,
  after a red tangle pulls straight; ✗ फसल ✗ मशीन → ✓ मानव श्रम उत्पादकता; capability blocks
  stacking into steps with a climbing arrow; two rising bars feeding शक्ति; two choice cards
  ticked "खुद तय"; the recap rebuilding the building row by row with each point's clip as a
  thumbnail, held lit 3.9 s; the big word "3 नंबर पक्के".
- **Transitions:** soft 0.6 s cross-fades. (The first cut had a T-square wipe, a bright rule running down
  the frame; the user removed it: "it creates a barrier and the student won't understand it".) The old
  drawing has left by the cut, and a pinned photo fades in only after it, so two screens never overlap.
- **Frame rate and motion (after the user's "laggy and stuck" review):** built at 25 fps, his clip's own rate
  (25 -> 30 showed every fifth frame of him twice); every clip enters 0.5 s in so a wipe never holds a
  first frame (13 stalls at cuts before); background blur halved (sigma 7.8) and a slow 5 % push-in on
  every toned shot, so the footage keeps moving under the drawings.
- **Slow, clear, one idea at a time (user review, same day):** entrances 1.8x the stage defaults, exits
  0.5 s; crowded scenes thinned (repeated labels cut, the ✗ boxes leave before the ✓ answer, the
  apprentice clip and the capability steps on separate screens); DESIGN_RULES §2 spacing measured with
  `tools/reel/spacing.mjs` and every flagged gap fixed. The recap kept as it was ("its good").
- **Sound:** a low sine drone in D on one clock with stone-hall air; stone thuds for pillars, ruler
  zips for dimension lines, a fine pen hiss for hand lettering, a glass chime for headings, a
  downward whoosh for the wipe, a deep settle when the building completes.
- **Veo:** 5 clips of manner (a pillared haveli courtyard; people of every kind through one open gate;
  an elder and a child planting a sapling; a master guiding an apprentice's hand plane; a young
  woman choosing a book).
- **Code:** `work/geo-c3-sa-01/design/` (kit.mjs, part1.mjs, sound.py, captions.json), build in
  `tools/reel/`, snapshot in `final/GEO-C3-SA-01/code/`.
